How it works

A guided cycle, not a one-off test

Hear a model

Start with a phrase or situation.

Respond aloud

Practise speaking naturally.

Assess

Use AI-supported interpretation and feedback.

Practise

Repeat or try a suitable exercise.

Build confidence

Apply skills to familiar situations.

Designed for Indian-language learners

For absolute beginners, native-language explanations can provide a bridge into English. As understanding develops, practice can increasingly focus on English itself.

Englighter's approach prioritises clear communication and learning progress rather than forcing every learner to imitate a particular accent.
