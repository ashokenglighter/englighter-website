Learning technology

AI that supports the learning process.

Englighter brings together speech recognition, pronunciation similarity analysis, language evaluation and adaptive workflows to support spoken-English practice.

The learning workflow

One connected practice loop.

The system uses signals from learner interactions to inform feedback and the next learning activity.

Speech input

The learner responds by speaking during an activity.

Speech processing

Speech recognition and voice activity detection support the interaction flow.

Performance signals

Pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary and fluency measures inform assessment.

Adaptive decision

The workflow can adjust difficulty, repetition and activity sequencing.

Personalised practice

The learner receives the next exercise, reinforcement or feedback.

Assessment approach

Focus on clarity and improvement.

Englighter’s methodology uses several complementary assessment signals rather than relying on a single measure.

〰️

Pronunciation similarity

MFCC features and Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) are used to compare speech patterns against reference audio, with learner calibration to account for natural variation.

🧠

Grammar and response quality

Language evaluation considers sentence structure, article use, verb agreement, tense consistency and the quality of a learner’s response.

📚

Vocabulary and fluency

Vocabulary targets and measures such as speaking rate, hesitation, response latency and speaking continuity contribute to the learning picture.

Assessment is intended to support clearer, understandable communication and progress relative to a learner’s starting point. It is not designed to demand imitation of a foreign accent.
Adaptive learning

Practice can change as the learner improves.

The methodology describes an adaptive loop that can respond to patterns in learner performance.

What can adapt?

  • Activity difficulty
  • Exercise sequencing
  • Repetition intensity
  • Corrective feedback
  • Progression decisions

Technology foundation

The methodology document describes speech recognition, AI-assisted language evaluation, Google text-to-speech, MFCC + DTW pronunciation similarity, n8n workflow orchestration and Firebase backend services.

These are implementation details; the learner-facing goal remains simple: more useful practice and clearer spoken communication.
