Privacy information

This page describes the basic handling of information through the Englighter public website. The application may involve additional processing that should be described in a separate, verified privacy notice before broad public release.

Website contact form: the form opens your device’s email application with your message prepared. The website itself does not send the form data to a website server.

Who operates this website?

Englighter is a brand of VQB Study Days Private Limited, based in Karnataka, India.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number and enquiry message. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information through a general enquiry.

How enquiries are used

Information sent to us may be used to respond to your enquiry, provide information you request and follow up on the subject of your message.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may make requests to that service when the pages load. The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Review the applicable provider policies and any future analytics, embedded content or form services before adding them.

Application data

This page is not a complete privacy notice for the Englighter application. Before using the application, users should be given an accurate explanation of the data processed by the live service, including speech processing, assessment and calibration data, retention, service providers and available user choices. Do not rely on this website notice as a statement that covers all app processing.
