How Englighter works

A learning loop that responds to the learner.

Rather than relying only on a fixed sequence, the learning workflow uses performance signals to help select the next activity and practice level.

Speak

The learner responds aloud during a guided activity.

Assess

The system evaluates relevant pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary and fluency signals.

Adapt

The next activity and difficulty can change based on performance.

Practise

The learner receives guided exercises and opportunities to try again.

Progress

Progression is informed by learning performance and level targets.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Who is Englighter designed for?

Englighter is designed for learners who want to improve spoken English, including absolute beginners and people who understand some English but need more speaking practice.

Do I need to know English already?

The foundational Englighter English Teacher pathway is designed for learners who may have little or no English understanding, using context and familiar-language support to get started.

How does the learning adapt?

The learning workflow considers learner performance and can adjust difficulty, activity sequencing, repetition and corrective feedback.

Does Englighter teach a foreign accent?

The methodology prioritises clear, understandable communication and improvement relative to a learner’s baseline—not imitation of a foreign accent.

How can I access the application?

Contact us for current availability and access instructions. This website does not assume that public app-store access is available.
