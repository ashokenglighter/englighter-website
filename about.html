About Englighter

Making spoken English practice more accessible

Englighter is an AI-supported spoken-English learning application developed by VQB Study Days Private Limited.

Our focus is to help Indian learners—especially those with little or no English understanding—build skills and confidence through guided, practical learning.

Our approach

Start with understanding. Build towards communication.

We believe learning should be structured, encouraging and relevant to real-life situations.

🌱

Start from the basics

Support learners who need a clear first step into English.

🖼️

Make meaning visible

Use images and familiar situations to support understanding.

🎧

Practise by speaking

Encourage listening and spoken responses as part of learning.

🧭

Progress gradually

Move through structured stages rather than expecting instant fluency.

🤝

Support different learners

Build a learning experience for varied starting points and needs.

💬

Focus on communication

Prioritise understandable, useful English for everyday life.
