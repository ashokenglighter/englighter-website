About Englighter

Making spoken-English practice more approachable.

Englighter is an AI-supported spoken-English learning platform built around a practical idea: learners need a clear path from understanding English to using it in conversation.

The application

Englighter combines a foundational learning pathway for people who may not yet understand English with a structured spoken-English pathway. The learning approach uses context, guided voice interaction, performance assessment and adaptive practice to help learners progress.

The learning philosophy

The platform prioritises understandable communication, learner confidence and improvement relative to a learner’s baseline. Its pronunciation methodology is designed to accommodate natural learner variation rather than require imitation of a foreign accent.

The company behind the platform

VQB Study Days Private Limited is the company behind the Englighter brand. The company is based in Karnataka, India.

Our focus

Support learners through structured, voice-first practice—from early understanding to more confident everyday spoken English.
